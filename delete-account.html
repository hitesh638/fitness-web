Delete your account

DEC Health Fitness Tracker (by DEC Health)

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open the app on your phone, or go to fitness.dechealth.com and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account, then Delete everything.

If the app asks you to sign in again first, do so and repeat step 3. This is a security check.

Option 2: by email

Email hitesh.mehta638@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

Your sign-in account, your profile (name, email, experience, goal, workouts per week), every workout you logged (exercises, sets, weights, reps, notes) and your custom exercises. Everything is deleted permanently from our servers. Nothing is kept.
