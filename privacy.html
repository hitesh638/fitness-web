Privacy Policy

DEC Health Fitness Tracker · Last updated 3 November 2026

DEC Health Fitness Tracker ("the app") helps you log your workouts and see your progress. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can delete it. It applies to the Android app and the website at fitness.dechealth.com.

Information we collect

WhatWhy
Name and email addressTo create and secure your account and show your name in the app.
Password (email sign-in only)To sign you in. It is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see it.
Google account name and email (if you use "Continue with Google")To sign you in.
Training experience, main goal and workouts per weekTo personalise the app, for example your weekly goal.
Workouts you log: exercises, sets, weights, reps, dates, times and notesTo save your history, show progress graphs and compare with last week.
Exercises you create yourselfSo you can use them again.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, health-app data, advertising ID or payment details. The app has no ads and no third-party analytics.

Where your data is stored

Your data is saved on your device so the app works offline, and in your account on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), hosted by Google in Mumbai, India. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Each account can only read and write its own data.

Workout reminders

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your device only. Your reminder times are not sent to our servers.

How we use your information

Only to run the app's features for you. We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with anyone for marketing. Google processes the data on our behalf as our hosting and sign-in provider.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. You can delete your account and all your data at any time:

  • In the app: Profile → Delete account. Everything is deleted from our servers and the device immediately.
  • Or follow the steps on our account deletion page.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

You can see and change your information in the app, and delete it as described above. For any other request, contact us.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
